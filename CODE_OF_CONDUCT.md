# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming and inclusive community.

## Our Pledge
Act and interact to foster an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards
- Use welcoming and inclusive language
- Be respectful of differing viewpoints
- Accept constructive criticism
- Focus on what is best for the project and community

## Enforcement
Report unacceptable behavior to maintainers via issues or direct contact. Consequences may include warnings or removal from participation.
